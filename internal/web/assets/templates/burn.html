How burns are counted

A burn is a send to a burn address. Nobody can ever receive it, so each burn stays as a receivable block forever. The total is the sum of those receivable blocks ({{group .Scan.BurnCount}} of them), counted straight from our node every hour. Sends from Banano-owned accounts in the known-accounts list count as team burns.
